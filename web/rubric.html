How the score is built

Five parts, out of 100
Mandatory 45
Skills 20
Experience 18
Qualifications 10
Certifications 7

Each part's weight is how much it can move the final number — a CV's score is just these parts added up.

Eligibility rules, recorded separately from scoring

These decide who is in the pool, not how high they score

National workforce preference

Nationals of the host country are advanced to review automatically. This is a lawful, approved hiring policy, applied openly and the same way every time.

It is not a hidden weight inside the score. It is a visible rule, recorded here, with an approver and a date.

Approved Visible Audited

Protected characteristics

Nationality beyond the rule above, gender, age, religion and family status are excluded from scoring entirely.

The rubric never sees them, so they cannot move a score up or down.
